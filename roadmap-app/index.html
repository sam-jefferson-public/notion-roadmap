How to use the roadmap
  • Each bar is one initiative, running left to right through time. It starts with a hatched discovery stretch and continues into a solid delivery block coloured by its RAG status.
  • Hover a bar to see whether its dates have moved since they were committed.
  • Click a bar to read what the initiative is, when it lands, its latest update, and to open it in Notion.
  • Milestones sit directly beneath the initiative they belong to, joined by a fine line.
